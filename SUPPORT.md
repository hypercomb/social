# Support Hypercomb

Hypercomb is free, open-source software and is not for sale. The complete
platform remains available under its existing open licenses whether or not
anyone contributes.

The project has been founder funded. Voluntary donations and sponsorship help
pay for continued development, testing, documentation, domains and hosting,
and may help the founder recover part of the time and money already invested
in creating Hypercomb.

Support does **not** purchase:

- equity or ownership in Hypercomb;
- repayment, interest, profit, or another financial return;
- governance power or control of the roadmap;
- guaranteed features, delivery dates, or private support;
- exclusive access or a closed edition of the software.

Supporters may choose to be acknowledged publicly, but acknowledgement is
optional and is not a reward with monetary value. Anonymous support is equally
welcome.

## Ways to help now

- Try [Hypercomb](https://hypercomb.io) and share what was clear or confusing.
- Share the [guided tour](https://hypercomb.com/tour/) with someone who cares
  about local-first or community-owned software.
- Study, run, or fork the [source](https://github.com/hypercomb/social).
- Introduce Hypercomb to a community that could use or test it.

A low-friction channel for one-time donations and recurring sponsorship is
being prepared. Until it is live, no one should send money through an
unpublished address or an account claiming to represent Hypercomb.

Contributions are not charitable donations and no charitable tax receipt is
offered unless a future funding page explicitly states otherwise.
