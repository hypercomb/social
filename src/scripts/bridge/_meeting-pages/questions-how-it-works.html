The tutorial and the Q and A

How it works

The tutorial plays itself. Then the whole room asks, answers and takes answers together.

In one minute

The host says start tutorial. A Q and A begins where they stand. Everyone taps Join and follows the host.

The explanation plays itself, a page every 45 seconds:

  1. Page 1Following
  2. Page 2The basics
  3. Page 3The idea
  4. Page 4Playing

When it ends, the whole room is walked into the questions together, and the game is on.

If you are a guest

  1. 01Join the room. Open the link or scan the code, and say yes to join.
  2. 02Follow the host. Tap Join on the game panel at the top of the screen — it reads … leads · Join. You now follow the host and go where they go.
  3. 03Ask. Tap Ask and type your question. Or say question and your question.
  4. 04Answer. Tap a question and write your answer in the notes that open.
  5. 05Take. Tick the answers you like. The most-taken answers win.
  6. 06Leave. Tap Leave, or say questions leave.

If you are the host

  1. 01Share the room. Share the room's link — the invite word makes it.
  2. 02Start. Say start tutorial.
  3. 03Give it a moment. Let people tap Join. The first page waits a little longer.
  4. 04Play along. You can ask or answer on any page while it plays.
  5. 05The questions. When it ends, the room is in the questions.
  6. 06Score. Say tally to score.
  7. 07Back. questions back returns to the page.

No tutorial today? start hypercomb q and a plays the game without it.

Walk

walk walks the page you stand on and every page beneath it, a stop every 45 seconds, round and round. Whoever follows you comes along.

walk every 30 sets the pace. walk stop stops.

To follow someone outside a game, open the people list — the small caret at the end of the row of initials at the top — and tap → beside their name. Tap it again to let go.
