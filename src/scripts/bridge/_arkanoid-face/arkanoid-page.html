Where it lives

Arkanoid is a beehaviour — a module riding above the Hypercomb hexagon grid, switchable like everything else in the hive.

Open the Hypercomb app and type /arkanoid (or /breakout, or /bricks) — the game mounts as a full-screen overlay above your tiles. /arkanoid design opens the level designer, where you paint bricks straight onto the grid and play what you drew.
