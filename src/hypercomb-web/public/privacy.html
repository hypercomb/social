Privacy

How Hypercomb handles your information. Last updated 1 September 2026.

Hypercomb is a local-first application. Your hive — every tile, note, picture and revision in it — is stored on your own device. There is no Hypercomb account, no sign-in, and no server that holds a copy of your work by default.

What we collect

Nothing. Hypercomb has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising identifiers. We do not operate a service that receives your data, and there is no account to attach data to. We never ask for your name, email address or payment details, because there is nothing to sign up for.

The application keeps local usage counters — for example, how often you have used a particular behaviour, so it can order menus by what you actually use. These counters are written into your own hive on your own disk and are never transmitted anywhere.

Where your hive lives

  • Desktop application. One database file inside your user profile, on your machine.
  • Web browser. The browser's private storage for the site (OPFS), on your machine.
  • Backups. Only in the folder you choose, when you choose it.

Uninstalling the desktop application, or clearing the site's storage in your browser, removes the hive from that device. Nothing is retained elsewhere on your behalf.

What leaves your device, and when

Every outbound connection Hypercomb makes is one you started. Nothing below happens on its own.

ActionWhat travelsWho can see it
You publish a page or branch to a host The content you selected, addressed by signature The host you chose, and anyone it serves that content to
You replicate content from a host Requests for the signatures you need That host sees your IP address and which signatures you asked for
You choose Serve This Hive Your machine accepts connections on a local port until you stop it Anyone who can reach your machine on your network
You join a shared room on the mesh Encrypted events for that room, via a relay The relay sees encrypted traffic; only people holding the room secret can read it
You use an AI behaviour The prompt and context you supplied The provider whose tool you connected — under their privacy terms, not ours

The mesh will not speak without a secret

Hypercomb refuses to subscribe to, or publish on, a shared room unless you have supplied both a room and a secret for it. Without both, no mesh traffic is sent or received. This is enforced in the application, not left to configuration.

Serving is off unless you turn it on

Serve This Hive makes your machine a host that other people's browsers and other Hypercomb nodes can read from. It runs only while you have it on, stops when you stop it or quit, and Windows will ask you to permit the connection the first time. Only content already published from your hive is served.

Third parties

The application makes no third-party requests of its own. Fonts, icons and libraries are shipped inside the application rather than fetched from a content delivery network, so simply opening Hypercomb contacts nobody.

Connections to hosts, relays and AI providers happen only where you have named one. Those services are operated by whoever runs them, under their own terms — including any host you run yourself. Hypercomb has no privileged view into them and receives nothing back from them.

Children

Hypercomb is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase on request. Your hive is in your hands: it can be exported to a folder in an open, self-verifying format at any time from Hive ▸ Back Up Hive…, and deleted by removing that folder and uninstalling the application.
