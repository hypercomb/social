# the flower

this is the heart of the story. jwize, 2026-10-28:

> The host represents the flower's reproductive system. When we publish we are expressing something. We are letting someone visit our flower. They come to your domain and then they replicate the content to their host. There is a pollination going on here, and when the host feels safe it allows the seed inside and the flower starts to grow.

a flower does not go looking for a bee. it opens. the opening is the expression. what leaves the flower is pollen, and pollen is not the plant. it is the plant's way of being carried. the bee does not become the flower it visited. it carries something home, and a flower that is ready receives it.

---

## the host is the reproductive system

the host is not a shelf and not a backup drive. it is the part of the flower that can receive and the part that can offer.

your own machine already holds what you have made. that is the comb, closed. publishing is the opening. you put the signed bytes where another person can stand in front of them. you are expressing something, and you are letting someone visit.

they come to your domain. they do not live there. they replicate what you expressed onto their own host. the copy is checked against its signature on the way in, the way a grain of pollen is still itself when it lands. what they hold is the same bytes. the flower those bytes grow in is theirs.

---

## pollination

pollination here is that crossing. someone visits your flower. something of yours leaves with them. it does not take root because they passed by. it takes root when their host feels safe.

safe is the receiving flower's own decision. the seed is allowed inside, and then the flower starts to grow. until that moment the visit is only a visit. the expression was real. the growth belongs to the host that admitted it.

funding a cell is a different act. that one is told in [the economy](the-economy.md). the contribution packet between domains, and the boundary that would judge it, is a design and is not what runs today. it is told in [the pollination protocol](../pollination-protocol.md). this chapter is the thing those words were reaching for: publish, visit, replicate, admit, grow.

---

## what is already true

the flower grows on what is already built.

- publishing is a human act. the bytes leave when you say so. a read-only site on a domain is the flower opened for a visit. it is not itself a hive that can grow.
- a visitor replicates by pulling. each byte is checked against its signature. an unpublished resource is still the only copy, and it stays on the device that wrote it.
- what travels is the expressed content: resources and layers, including a page. what stays is the key, the host credentials, and the private record.
- admission is a choice of the receiving host. following a host, and turning updates on, is that choice. bringing a trial page home is that choice too: a trusted hand, and a confirm. a model does not carry the seed in.

when the host feels safe, the seed is inside, and the flower starts to grow. that growth is their hive, on their domain, from what your flower expressed.
