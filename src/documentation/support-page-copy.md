# Funding page copy

This is the source text for a GitHub Sponsors or Ko-fi page. Keep the substance
identical across platforms so no supporter receives a different promise.

## Headline

Keep Hypercomb free, open and in the hands of its users

## Short description

Hypercomb is free, open-source, community-owned software where your work lives
with you. Voluntary support helps me continue building it and recover part of
the founder-funded cost of getting it this far.

## Full description

Hypercomb starts from a simple premise: your work should not depend on an
account, a central platform, or a company's continued permission. A hive lives
with its participant. Published content is carried by signature and verified
by whoever receives it. The source is AGPL and the documentation is Creative
Commons.

I have funded the work so far myself. If you want Hypercomb to keep developing,
you can support my time, testing, documentation, domains, hosting and build
infrastructure—and help me recover part of what I have already invested in
creating it.

This is support, not a purchase or financial investment. A contribution does
not buy equity, repayment, profit, governance power, guaranteed features,
exclusive access or a private edition. Hypercomb stays free and open whether
or not you contribute.

The best place to start is the working software at https://hypercomb.io and
the guided tour at https://hypercomb.com/tour/.

Thank you for helping an alternative exist.

## Tiers

All tiers fund the same free public project. The names are acknowledgements,
not bundles of exclusive benefits.

- **$5 one time — Support the hive.** A simple thank-you. Anonymous is welcome.
- **$15 monthly — Keep the hive running.** Sustains ordinary project costs.
- **$50 monthly — Sustain the work.** Helps reserve consistent development
  time. Optional acknowledgement on a future supporters page.
- **Custom — Contribute what is comfortable.** One time or recurring, with no
  additional promise attached.

## Funding-use statement

Funds may be used for the founder's development time, project hosting and
domains, build and signing infrastructure, testing devices and services,
documentation and communication, and recovery of prior founder-funded project
costs. Material project progress will remain public. Individual personal or
financial details will not be published.

## Checkout note

This contribution is voluntary. It is not a purchase, loan, security,
charitable gift, or promise of a financial return. No charitable tax receipt
is provided.
